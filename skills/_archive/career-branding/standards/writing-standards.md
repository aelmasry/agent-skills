# Writing Standards

## Voice

- Professional, clear, confident — not boastful.
- First person for LinkedIn About; third person only when user requests (e.g., executive bio).
- Avoid: "passionate professional", "results-driven", "synergy", "rockstar", "ninja".

## Structure

- Lead with role + value, not tool lists.
- First 2–3 lines of LinkedIn About must carry keywords (visible before "see more").
- Bullets: **Action verb + scope + method/technology + outcome** (when facts support it).

## Quantification

- Use real metrics from `memory/achievements-log.md`.
- Unknown metrics → `[add metric]` or ask the user.

## Keyword Use

- Mirror target JD vocabulary naturally when tailoring.
- Introduce acronyms with full term once (e.g., "Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)").
- Do not list technologies without context.

## Anti-Patterns

- Tool dumps without business context
- Duplicate bullets across roles
- Generic summaries that could apply to any engineer
- Dating projects as "Present" when work ended years ago

## Cross-Document Consistency

Titles, dates, and employers must match across resume, LinkedIn, and cover letter.
