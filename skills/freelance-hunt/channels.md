# Channels — Jobs, Freelance, Potential Clients

| Channel | Best for | Avoid |
|---------|----------|-------|
| **Referrals / network** | FT, PT, clients — highest close rate | Ignoring warm intros |
| **LinkedIn** (search + Easy Apply sparingly + Services) | Discoverability; inbound clients; selected FT | Spray Easy Apply without tailored CV |
| **Company career pages** | FT / PT with clear JD | Blind portals when no fit |
| **Warm outbound** (personalized, problem-first) | Potential clients; hiring managers after engagement | Generic “I’m available” |
| **Upwork / similar** | Smaller freelance scopes | Race-to-bottom vague briefs |
| **Specialized networks** (e.g. Toptal-class) | Curated senior contract | Assuming acceptance |
| **Job boards** (LinkedIn Jobs, etc.) | FT / PT volume with filter | Applying without `cv-jd-matcher` |

## Practical cadence

1. Keep LinkedIn discoverable (`linkedin-optimizer`).
2. Score every lead/JD with the scorecard — reply fast only to Go / Negotiate.
3. **Jobs (FT/PT):** Go → `cv-jd-matcher` before submit.
4. **Freelance:** Go → short proposal (phased).
5. **Potential client:** Go → 1 personalized note + ask for a 20-min discovery call.
6. Prefer 1 strong package over 10 mediocre applications.

## Client outreach (short)

```
[Context: what you noticed about their product/problem — one specific line]

I help teams with [production AI / architecture / backend outcome].
Relevant: [one proof].

Open to a short call to see if a focused engagement makes sense?
```

Tag people only when relevant. No link-stuffed cold spam.
