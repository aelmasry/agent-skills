# Interview Standards

## Preparation Structure

1. Review `memory/professional-identity.md` and `memory/achievements-log.md`
2. Build STAR stories for top 5–8 achievements
3. Prepare role-specific technical and leadership narratives
4. Align stories to target JD when provided

## STAR Format

- **Situation** — context (1–2 sentences)
- **Task** — your responsibility
- **Action** — what you did (specific, technical where relevant)
- **Result** — measurable outcome when possible

## Story Selection

Prioritize stories demonstrating:

- System design and architecture decisions
- Production reliability and scale
- AI/ML integration in real systems (if factual)
- Technical leadership and mentoring
- Cross-functional delivery and business impact

## Answer Length

- Behavioral: 90–120 seconds spoken (~150–250 words written)
- Leadership: emphasize trade-offs, stakeholders, outcomes
- Technical: depth appropriate to seniority; connect to business value

## Honesty

- Do not fabricate stories or metrics
- Flag gaps honestly; prepare bridging narratives from `memory/`
