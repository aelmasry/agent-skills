# Content Creation Standards

## Purpose

Support professional visibility: technical posts, conference takeaways, architecture insights, leadership perspectives.

## Positioning Alignment

Content must reinforce `memory/professional-identity.md` — AI engineering, architecture, production systems, leadership.

## Structure (default post)

1. Hook — insight or question, not hype
2. Context — why it matters for practitioners
3. 3–5 concrete takeaways
4. Closing — invitation to discuss or link to resource

## Tone

- Practitioner-first; avoid marketing fluff
- Share trade-offs and lessons learned, not only successes
- Credit sources and teams when appropriate

## Do Not

- Claim work not in `memory/achievements-log.md`
- Publish confidential employer or client details without approval
- Default to data-platform content unless user requests

## Formats

- LinkedIn post: 150–300 words (short) or 400–600 (long form)
- Article outline: problem → approach → results → lessons
- Talk abstract: audience, outcome, 3 key points

Use `prompts/content/` and `templates/content/` when available.
