# LinkedIn Optimizer

Follow repo `DISCUSSION-MODE.md`. Until Ali asks to rewrite: short audit,
two Headline options, one question. Do not emit a full About + Experience
pack or a content calendar unsolicited.

## Goal

Improve **recruiter and client discoverability** for:

- AI Engineering
- Solutions / Software Architecture
- Backend Engineering
- Technical Leadership
- **Jobs + clients inbound** (FT/PT recruiters and consulting — CTA matches `target-profile.md`)

Optimize for reputation and relevance — not vanity likes.

## Inputs required

| Required | Optional |
|----------|----------|
| Current Headline + About (paste) | Full Experience sections |
| Target lane (AI / Arch / Backend) | Skills list / Featured |
| | Master resume for fact alignment |

## Workflow

1. **Audit** — Headline, About, Experience, Skills, Featured against [visibility-checklist.md](visibility-checklist.md)
2. **Keywords** — Recruiter-searchable phrases from [keyword-clusters-ai-arch.md](../cv-jd-matcher/keyword-clusters-ai-arch.md) (truthful only)
3. **Rewrite sections** — Copy-ready Headline (2 options), About, current-role bullets, Top 3 Skills to pin. Method: [references/section-rewrite.md](references/section-rewrite.md)
4. **Featured strategy** — 2–3 high-signal items (case study, repo, article, cert)
5. **Freelance signal** — Clear consulting CTA if freelance-first (Services + About last line)
6. **Visibility plan** — Max **2 posts/week**; topic seeds only here. If Ali asks for a week calendar, hand off to `content-matrix` Step 5. Drafts are `social-content-system`.

## Rewrite method (when asked)

Details: [references/section-rewrite.md](references/section-rewrite.md)

- **Headline A (search):** `[Primary title] | [Specialty] | [Outcome or domain]`
- **Headline B (consulting):** `[What you do] | [Who you help] [result]` — still one searchable role noun
- **About:** 7-step (hook → role → proof → value → human → specialties → CTA). First two lines = keywords. Hook is a claim or number, not a question.
- **Experience:** `action verb + specific metric` when the metric is resume-true; otherwise concrete scope, never invented numbers

## Non-negotiable rules

- Align LinkedIn with resume facts — no contradictions
- One primary lane in Headline (not three equal titles)
- No buzzword salad ("passionate", "guru", "disruptor")
- Prefer production AI + architecture language over demo hype
- Do not promise view/interview multipliers as facts
